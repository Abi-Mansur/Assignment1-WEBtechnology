Paul Walker

12.03.1973 - 30.11.2013

Biography

Paul William Walker IV was an American actor and philanthropist, best known for his role as Brian O'Conner in the "Fast & Furious" franchise. Born on September 12, 1973, in Glendale, California, Walker began his career as a child actor in the 1980s and gained fame in the late 1990s. He was also known for his humanitarian work, particularly with the organization Reach Out Worldwide. Tragically, Walker passed away in a car accident on November 30, 2013, leaving behind a legacy of memorable performances and charitable contributions.
